# Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban Recomp: setup

Thank you first of all to **MiddleDucky**, whose recompilation work the Prisoner of Azkaban PC port is built on. It
is a brilliant piece of work, and every check here stands on it.

## What you need

- The PC port (hppoa.exe and its launcher) built from the Archipelago branch, with your own copy of the Xbox game.
- Archipelago 0.6.8 or newer, with this world's `hppoa.apworld` in its `custom_worlds` folder.

## Status

The world generates seeds; the game side (connecting, sending checks, receiving items) is still being built. Until
it lands, a seed is useful for testing the generator only.
