# Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban Recomp

With warm thanks to **MiddleDucky**, whose recompilation work the Prisoner of Azkaban PC port stands on.

## What is randomized

- Chapters: every chapter after Night 0 has a pass. The seed starts on Day 1 with the Intro, Hogwarts Express and
  Night 0 behind you; the pause menu's Archipelago page travels to any chapter whose pass you hold.
- Areas: the challenge rooms, the Shrieking Shack, the Quidditch match, the Dementor battle, the Dark Tower, the
  House Cup and the bonus activities each need an area pass on top of their chapter.
- Spells: the nine spells after Flipendo are items, and their spellbooks are checks.
- Story tokens: one per chapter; the Dark Tower goal needs a set number of them.
- Check slop: Minimal (story only), Standard (adds cards, card sets, Folio Bruti, Lost and Found, statues, potions,
  bonus activities, Marauder's Challenges, Fred and George, the shop, secrets and milestones) or Maximum; every group
  can be switched on or off on its own.

## Goal

Fly Buckbeak to the Dark Tower (with enough story tokens, the Night 5 and Dark Tower passes and Expecto Patronum),
attend the House Cup, or own a set number of collector's cards.
