# Handoff 2026-10-30: 60 fps (and 120+) at the game's speed

Branch `m3-60fps` = local `m3-widescreen` + 0b504e6 (not pushed; ask Austin before pushing). Evidence:
docs/bringup.md, "60 fps".

- **What it was:** not frame-stepped logic. PoA's frame timer `sub_001A7450` (this 0x38BA6C) adds
  max(real elapsed, floor) to the game clock each frame; the floor (+0x18) is 33 ms on NTSC from renderer init. About
  70 call sites read that clock (`sub_001A8C30`). At 60 fps every frame gained 33 ms: 2x speed.
- **Fix:** `port/src/hle_video.c` overrides `sub_001A7450` and sets the floor to 1 ms when `fps_limit` > 30 (670026f; 600/fps caused false 100 ms steps, Austin play60)
  (game's own value at 0/30); `Pace` gives one vblank a frame at 60. Choices 60, 120-240, unlocked are back in the F10
  panel and launcher (undoes c6ba8d4's clamp). Default stays the game's rate (30 fps). A `[CLOCK]` log line every 10 s
  shows game ms vs real ms.
- **Runs f60a-f60d** (30/60/60/120 fps, `poa_newgame.pad` via `Harry Potter Xbox Decomps\tools\poarun.ps1`): 59-60 /
  115-120 fps in the Hogwarts Express, game clock x1.00-1.03 at every rate, level audio at 60 lines up with 30 at scale
  1.00. 0 ICALL / CRASH.
- **Next:** Austin play-tests at 60 (walk/run speed, animations, particles, spells, movies, cutscenes) with his own
  controller. If it holds, consider making 60 the default (`fps_limit` default in app.h / launcher), as CD1 did.
  Watch for anything still timed per frame: candidates would be the 30 real-clock readers (`sub_001A7520`) and the two
  1/60 floats (file offsets 0x2C6E78, 0x2D32C0), not traced. Widescreen (311a8c2) is independent of this.
- **Update (play-test):** Austin's first 60 fps play (play60) felt odd and held back Flippendo's sounds: false 100 ms
  clock steps from the 10 ms floor. Fixed in 670026f (1 ms floor); Austin's second play (play60b): "Good." Pending:
  his go to push, and whether 60 becomes the default.
